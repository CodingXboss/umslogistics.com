About the Company

Built by Drivers. Run for Drivers.

UMS Logistics Inc. was founded in 2020 with one mission: deliver freight reliably while treating drivers and partners the way the industry should have been treating them all along.

From a small operation to a fleet of 40+ trucks running coast to coast, we've built our growth on consistent miles, transparent statements, and dispatch that picks up the phone — every time.

  • Direct relationships with major brokerages — better rates passed to drivers.
  • Owner operators, company drivers, lease owners & team drivers — all welcome.
  • Dedicated round-trip lanes with consistent freight & great mileage per week.
